# Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard analysing coffee shop sales performance, revenue trends, product categories and store operations.

## Business Questions Answered

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which store location performs best?
- What hours generate the highest transaction activity?
- How is revenue trending over time?
- What operational patterns exist across locations?

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualisation
- Business Intelligence
- Data Modelling

## Key Insights

- Coffee generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Hell’s Kitchen recorded the strongest overall store performance.
- Transaction activity peaked during morning business hours.
- Revenue showed a steady upward trend over time.
